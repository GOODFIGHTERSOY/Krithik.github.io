02 / Traffic Demand Prediction
10.4%
lower MAE after feature engineering
Traffic Demand Prediction — KS

Machine learning · Hackathon project

A better read on traffic demand.

Traffic patterns depend on context. I engineered time, road, lane, weather and geographic features, then trained XGBoost to predict demand.

R² 0.857 on 15,460 validation samples. MAE 0.036; RMSE 0.054.

The story behind it: Traffic Demand Prediction
The question
Can road and time context improve a traffic-demand model?
What I built
A workflow covering missing values, categorical encoding and contextual feature engineering, including peak-hour and night-time signals.
The evidence
Feature engineering reduced MAE by 10.4% and RMSE by 10.9%. The solution qualified for Round 2 of Flipkart Gridlock 2.0.
What the numbers describe
These are validation results for the dataset, not a claim of measured improvement on a live road network.
